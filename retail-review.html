Retail Catalogue Quality Review

When purchasing orders ten cases, how many individual units should the stock report show? This fictional Excel case study compares a retail catalogue with a supplier file before pack sizes are used for reporting.

Download Excel workbook

What the checks found

13 of 18 catalogue products passed. Five need investigation: two pack sizes disagree, one product has no supplier match, one matches two supplier rows, and one has no catalogue pack size.

Follow one product

Orange juice (SKU R101) is listed as 6 units per case in the catalogue and 12 in the supplier file. For an order of 10 cases that is 60 versus 120 units. The check identifies the discrepancy. The product steward requests the signed specification before approving a change.

How to explore the workbook

  1. Open Catalogue and Supplier to find R101 in both sources. SKU is the product identifier.
  2. In Checks, COUNTIF counts matching supplier rows. XLOOKUP retrieves a pack size only when the match is unique. Each catalogue product receives one result.
  3. In Issue Log, find the evidence, assigned role and next action. Status is maintained manually.
  4. In a practice copy, assume a specification confirms 12 units. Change cell D2 in Catalogue from 6 to 12. The Dashboard should show four products needing review. Record the resolution separately in the issue log.

Scope: this exercise checks the 18 supplied catalogue rows against the supplier file. A pass means these pack-size checks succeeded.
