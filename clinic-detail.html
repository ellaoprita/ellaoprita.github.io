GOVERNANCE CASE STUDY

From clinic records to trusted reporting.

A proposed operating model for the same fictional clinic network. The goal is to make the appointment report traceable and give someone clear responsibility when a check fails.

Ownership and access

Data owner · Clinic operations manager
Approves definitions, access and publication of the report.
Data steward · Scheduling lead
Reviews exceptions and coordinates corrections with the booking team.
Reporting analyst
Runs quality checks and uses only the fields the report needs.

Issue handling

  1. Flag the affected rows and record which rule failed.
  2. Assign the issue to the scheduling lead to verify against the booking system.
  3. Correct the source, rerun the checks and record the resolution. Keep unresolved rows out of reporting.
