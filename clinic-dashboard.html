Which bookings can we count?

Across three fictional clinics, 82 of 1,862 source records failed at least one quality check. The remaining 1,780 bookings form the basis of the attendance report.

Inspect the checks in SQLite

Group rows by appointment ID to find repeated bookings. The quality view flags later occurrences in load order and preserves the source rows for investigation.

SELECT appointment_id, COUNT(*) AS occurrences
FROM booking_raw
GROUP BY appointment_id
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1;

Attendance across the network

Of 1,780 reportable bookings, 1,427 were attended, 224 were missed and 129 were cancelled. The missed-visit rate across all clinics was 13.6%.

Who resolves a flagged record?

In this proposed workflow, the scheduling lead checks the flagged booking against the source system. The clinic operations manager approves changes to the rules and signs off on access. The reporting analyst reruns the checks before releasing the figures.
