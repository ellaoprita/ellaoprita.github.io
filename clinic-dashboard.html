SQL method and governance decisions

The raw export preserves load order. SQL checks required fields, validates visit outcomes and uses ROW_NUMBER by appointment ID to flag later occurrences. The reporting view excludes flagged rows. The two monthly SQL views feed the charts. Export month is retained even when the visit date is missing.

Proposed workflow: the scheduling lead checks exceptions against the booking system. The clinic operations manager approves rule changes. The reporting analyst reruns the checks and reconciles raw, flagged and reportable counts.
